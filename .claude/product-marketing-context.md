## 1. Product overview

**One-liner** 🟢
سالیکو — تولیدکننده تشک و محصولات خواب با بیش از دو دهه تجربه.
*Salyco — mattress and sleep-products manufacturer with over two decades of experience.*

**What it does** 🟢
Salyco manufactures and sells mattresses direct to consumers in Iran, plus through a
dealer network. Every mattress carries a **120-month (10-year) warranty** with an
online registration and admin-approval flow. Two models are live.

**Category (the shelf)** 🟢 — `تشک` / mattresses and sleep products.
Customers search: `تشک طبی`, `تشک فنری`, `بهترین تشک`, `قیمت تشک`, `خرید تشک`.

**Type** 🟢 — Manufacturer + D2C e-commerce + dealer distribution. Vertically
integrated (they make what they sell — a real trust asset most Iranian mattress
sites cannot claim).

**Business model** 🟢 — One-time product purchase, no subscription.

| Model | List | After discount | Warranty | Firmness |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| مدل اِمپریال (Emperial) | 27,000,000 ﷼ | 24,300,000 (−10%) | 120 mo | 5 |
| مدل پرستیژ (Prestige) | 19,000,000 ﷼ | 16,150,000 (−15%) | 120 mo | 3 |

Positioned in the **premium** tier of the Iranian market. 🟡

**Location** 🟢 — نیشابور، خیابان مدرس، خیابان فضل — Khorasan Razavi, Iran.
Hours: شنبه تا پنج‌شنبه، ۹ تا ۲۰.

## 2. Target audience

**Primary** 🟡 — Iranian households buying a mattress as a considered, infrequent,
relatively high-value purchase (16–27 M ﷼). Mattresses are replaced every 8–10
years, so nearly every buyer is re-entering a category they last researched a
decade ago and have low confidence in.

**Secondary** 🟢 — Regional retailers and dealers (a `/dealers` route and a
national dealer network exist).

**Decision dynamics** 🔴 — Likely joint household decision, often with one partner
researching and both approving. **Unconfirmed.**

**Jobs to be done** 🟡
1. *"Help me stop waking up in pain"* — health-driven, urgent, highest intent.
2. *"Help me buy this once and not think about it for ten years"* — durability-driven;
   the 120-month warranty speaks directly to this.
3. *"Help me not get ripped off"* — the category is opaque; buyers cannot evaluate
   what is inside a mattress and know it.

**Use cases** 🟡 — back/disc pain relief · replacing a worn mattress · furnishing a
new home or marriage · upgrading to a premium sleep setup.

## 4. Problems and pain points

**Core challenge** 🟡 — Buyers cannot evaluate mattresses. Specs are opaque or
absent, `طبی` vs `فنری` is poorly understood, firmness is unstandardized, and the
consequence of choosing wrong is a decade of bad sleep plus a large sunk cost.

**Why alternatives fall short** 🟡
- Marketplace listings (Digikala et al.) have no manufacturer accountability and
  warranties that are hard to enforce.
- Physical showrooms give a 5-minute lie-down as the entire evaluation, then apply
  sales pressure.
- Most competing brands publish thin specs, so comparison is impossible.

**What it costs them** 🟡 — Chronic pain, unrefreshing sleep, and 16–27 M ﷼ that
cannot be recovered if the choice is wrong.

**Emotional tension** 🟡 — *"I'll spend a month's income on something I can't
evaluate, and I'll live with the mistake for ten years."* This is the emotional
core the copy should address. It currently does not.

## 6. Differentiation

**Verified differentiators** 🟢
1. **120-month warranty with a working registration and approval system** — not a
   marketing claim; there is real software behind it (`WarrantyRegistration`,
   `WarrantyRequestsPanel`, admin approval flow). **This is the strongest asset and
   the most under-marketed.**
2. **Manufacturer, not reseller** — 20+ years, a real Neyshabur address, own
   production lines.
3. **Enamad certified** (نماد اعتماد الکترونیکی) — present in the footer; the
   baseline trust signal for Iranian e-commerce.
4. **National dealer network** — buyers can see and touch before buying.

**Claimed but not yet substantiated on-site** 🟡 — anti-allergy foams, breathable
fabrics, ergonomic design, engineered firmness. All asserted in About copy; **none
appear in product specs** (`material` is empty on both products). Substantiate or
stop claiming.

**Why it's better** — Ten years of warranty from a manufacturer that will still
exist in ten years, versus a marketplace listing whose seller may not.

## 11. Style guide 🟢

**Grammar and mechanics**
- Persian throughout; RTL. Use ZWNJ (`‌`) correctly in compounds: `می‌گیرد`,
  `دسته‌بندی`, `تنفس‌پذیر` — never a plain space, never omitted.
- Persian numerals in prose (`۱۲۰ ماه`); Latin digits acceptable in specs and prices.
- Prices in تومان with thousands separators: `۲۴,۳۰۰,۰۰۰ تومان`.
- Warranty always in months, matching the API: `۱۲۰ ماه` / `۱۰ سال`.
- Avoid Arabic `ي`/`ك`; use Persian `ی`/`ک`.

**Capitalization and naming**
- Brand is always `سالیکو` in Persian body copy — never `برند ما`, never transliterated.
- Latin model names in Title Case inside parentheses on first mention:
  `مدل اِمپریال (Emperial)`, `مدل پرستیژ (Prestige)`. Persian name alone thereafter.
- Latin brand name is `Salyco` — never `SALYCO` or `salyco` outside URLs.

**Formatting**
- One `<h1>` per page, and it must contain `تشک` and/or `سالیکو` (see plan §3.3).
- Article bodies must use real `<h2>`/`<h3>`, real `<ol>`/`<ul>` — never numerals
  typed as text (see `salyco-site-audit.md` §8).
- Sentence-length target: under 25 words. Break long Persian sentences.
- Tables for anything comparative (sizes, firmness by weight, model comparison).

**Typography and color**
- Typeface: **Vazirmatn** — self-host it (see plan §1.8). `Salyco-DESIGN.md`
  incorrectly specifies Inter; the site loads Vazirmatn. **Vazirmatn is correct.**
- Brand navy `#003087`. **Never use gold `#F5BA2E` for text on white** — fails
  contrast.
